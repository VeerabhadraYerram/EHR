# BodyParts3D atlas assets

BodyParts3D, Database Center for Life Science (DBCLS). Licensed under CC BY 4.0.
https://dbarchive.biosciencedbc.jp/en/bodyparts3d/lic.html

Converted from the official PART-OF and ISA OBJ archives into GLB, grouped by organ/system, and vertex normals recalculated. Original coordinate frame retained; high-density surfaces simplified with documented 2 mm whole-body / 0.7 mm organ vertex clustering. Patient clinical states do not turn these reference meshes into scan-derived anatomy.
